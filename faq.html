Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about mechanical engineering? Start here.

Mechanical engineers design, build and fix things that move: engines, robots, bicycles, lifts, pumps and even the fans in your laptop. They use maths and science to make sure a machine is strong, safe and does its job well.

Yes, but you don't need to be a genius. You need to be willing to practise. Engineers use maths every day to measure forces, speeds and sizes, and the more you practise, the easier it gets.

Mathematics and Physics are the most important. Chemistry, Computer Studies and Drawing & Design are very helpful too.

Take things apart (with permission!), build models, join a robotics or coding club, and learn to code. You're already doing that by building this website.
